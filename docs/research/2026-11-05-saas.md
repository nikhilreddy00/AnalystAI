## 1. Grain of typical extracts

- **Subscription table** (one row per subscription: customer_id, plan, amount, interval,
  start_date, end/cancel date, status). The most common upload. MRR at a date = sum of active
  rows' normalised amounts; movements need two snapshots or start/end dates.
- **Subscription-item / line-item rows** (Stripe: "each subscription item corresponding to an
  invoice line item" [SR]). A row is an item, not a customer: Stripe counts "a customer with
  multiple active subscriptions … as a single active subscriber" [SA]; ChartMogul ARPA counts "a
  customer with multiple subscriptions … once" [CMA].
- **Invoice table** (invoice_id, customer, line items, service period, amount, tax, discount).
  MRR "cannot be calculated by simply summing invoice totals" [SS]; needs the service period
  and recurring flag per line [CMM, SR].
- **MRR-per-customer-per-month** (Stripe prebuilt report "MRR per subscriber per month … at the
  end of each month" [SA]) and **MRR movement logs** ("Customer MRR changes … new subscribers,
  upgrades, downgrades, reactivations, and churn" [SA]). The best grain for bridges and cohorts.
- **Telco/consumer churn samples** (one row per customer, a `Churn` Yes/No flag, tenure months,
  monthly charges, **no dates**): like the IBM HR sample, a share of rows, not a period churn rate.

## 2. Definitions (exact)

Notation: MRR(t) = MRR at date t; C(t) = paying customers at t; N, E, R, X, K = new, expansion,
reactivation, contraction, churned MRR in the period.

### 2.1 MRR
- Stripe: "the sum of the monthly-normalized value of all your `active` and `past_due`
  subscriptions. MRR calculations exclude any taxes …, any subscribers on free plans, and any
  metered (usage based) products. When a subscriber's subscription is canceled or marked as
  `unpaid`, then the subscription is considered churn" [SA]. Example: 100 × $100/mo + 50 ×
  ($600/yr ÷ 12) = $12,500 [SA].
- ChartMogul: "the sum of recurring revenue across all active subscriptions" incl. past due;
  "One-time (non-recurring) line items don't" count; "MRR is offset by discounts"; non-monthly
  plans normalised "using the invoice amount and the billing interval" [CMM].
- Baremetrics: "the value of all active subscription customers"; "All non-monthly plans are
  normalized to monthly … $120 on an annual plan, we add $10 to MRR for the next 12 months" [BM].
- ProfitWell: "we ignore any one-time customer transactions"; MRR "exclusive of taxes" [PF].
- Counting unit: currency; as-of rule: subscriptions active **on** the date (point-in-time).
  Month-end MRR is the convention for monthly series (Stripe "at the end of each month" [SA]).

In / out, by source:
| Item | Stripe | ChartMogul | Baremetrics | ProfitWell |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| One-time / setup fees | out ("past one off invoices cannot be relied upon" [SS]) | out [CMM] | — | out [PF] |
| Taxes | out [SA, SS] | deducted when the line states tax (snippet, CMM family) | — | out [PF] |
| Discounts | configurable; "Permanent recurring discounts, are always subtracted" [SA] | subtracted [CMM]; applying one = Contraction, expiry = Expansion [CMV] | subtracted; 100% coupon = free user / churn [BM] | "always subtract discounts and coupons … regardless of frequency" [PF] |
| Usage / metered | out ("Usage MRR" in preview) [SA] | — | — | "roll all recurring metered billing into MRR" [PC] |
| Trials | trialing items out [SA, SS] | — | — | out, incl. paid trials; 100% discount = trial [PF] |
| Free plans | out [SA] | out of ARPA [CMA] | free↔paid = new / cancellation [BM] | "bundle free plans into trials" [PC] |
| Past due / delinquent | in until `unpaid`/canceled [SA] | in (Past due) [CMM, CMN] | churned after 30 days delinquent [BM] | "delinquently churned" [PF] |
| Paused | — | — | — | $0 MRR [PC]; shown as voluntary churn on renewal date [PF] |
| Refunds | one-off refunds don't affect MRR [SS] | deducted for Stripe sources only [CMM] | — | "Generally … ignore refunds" [PS] |
| FX | FX adjustment line in MRR growth [SA] | rate "on the service period start date"; no expansion/contraction from FX, separate "Exchange Rate Impact" row [CMF] | — | — |

### 2.2 ARR, committed/contracted ARR, run rate
- ARR = MRR × 12 [SS]. a16z: ARR "should exclude one-time (non-recurring) fees and professional
  service fees" [A16].
- a16z trap: "people will multiply one month's all-in bookings by 12 to get to ARR. Common
  mistakes … (1) counting non-recurring fees such as hardware, setup, installation … (2) counting
  bookings" [A16].
- Contracted / committed ARR (CARR): contracted ARR "whether in production or not yet in
  production"; "should not be used interchangeably" with ARR or bookings [SMSB, snippet, draft
  v0.9]. Sources disagree on subtracting known (notified) churn: FounderPath subtracts it,
  Hyperline's narrower definition does not (vendor glossaries, snippet; **unverified**).
  → A subscription file usually holds only live subscriptions: report ARR, not CARR, unless a
  signed-not-started column exists.

### 2.3 MRR movements and the bridge
- ChartMogul types: New Business, Expansion, Contraction, Churn, Reactivation, Neutral [CMV]:
  - New Business: a lead's first subscription [CMV, CML].
  - Expansion: "When a customer's overall MRR increases"; "can also result from an expiring
    discount" [CMV].
  - Contraction: downgrade or cancelling "one or more (but not their last) subscription"; "can
    also result from applying a discount" [CMV].
  - Churn: "When a customer cancels their last (or only) subscription" [CMV]; recorded "at the end
    of the current billing period" [CML] (configurable "Churn Recognition" [CMV]).
  - Reactivation: a former paying customer "moves back onto a paid plan"; **no time window** [CMV].
    Cancel then buy a dearer plan = Churn + Reactivation, not Expansion [CMV].
- Stripe "MRR growth": start MRR + new + reactivation + expansion − contraction − churn ± FX
  adjustment; worked example 1,000 + 100 + 50 − 40 − 60 − 5 = 1,045 [SA]. (Stripe's own example
  labels the churn line "downgrade from Plan B": a doc wording slip; the amount is a full churn.)
- Stripe "Churned revenue" = churned MRR + contraction MRR [SA] (a gross loss figure).
- ProfitWell: cancelled MRR is "recognized until the end of their billing cycle"; Stripe removes it
  "at the time of cancellation" [PS]. Reactivation: "If a customer churns and comes back within
  any period in time" [PC]; Stripe: "Since the subscription has been deleted, it cannot be
  reactivated" [PS, older page]. Maxio uses a 90-day window (as described on PC, **unverified**
  against Maxio's own docs).
- Same-month signup + churn: ProfitWell "ignore[s] them from your metrics entirely to not inflate
  new + churn" [PS, PF]; Stripe "factored into both new MRR and Churn" [PS].
- **Bridge identity** (pack rule): MRR(end) = MRR(start) + N + E + R − X − K (± FX if
  multi-currency, as its own line [SA, CMF]). Must reconcile to the cent; a residual means a
  movement is missing or double-counted. Classification is per **customer**, not per row
  (ChartMogul: net change per customer → Expansion or Contraction [CMV]).

### 2.4 Logo (customer) churn rate
- ChartMogul standard: numerator = customers who churned in the period − those who "joined and
  churned in the period" − those who "churned and reactivated in the period"; denominator = "the
  number of customers at the start of the period". Example (12 − 2) ÷ 100 = 10% [CMC]. Metrics
  library: churned ÷ "customers at the start of that period" [CMB].
- ChartMogul legacy (B2C, accounts before 2022-08-15): sum of daily rates (5% + 5.26% = 10.26%)
  [CMC]: a different number on the same data.
- Stripe: "churned subscribers in the past 30 days, divided by the number of active subscribers 30
  days ago, plus the total new subscribers in the past 30 days": 100 ÷ (1,000 + 100) = 9.1% [SA].
  → Same data, ChartMogul-style = 10.0%, Stripe = 9.1%.
- Partial period: ChartMogul extrapolates "(total days ÷ days passed) × real churn rate" [CMC].
  Pack: do not extrapolate; report the completed period only.
- Free/trial accounts are not customers: Stripe active subscribers "doesn't include subscribers on
  free plans or trials" [SA]; ChartMogul separates "All Subscriber" vs "Paid Subscriber" churn
  (snippet, help article 308).

### 2.5 Revenue churn, GRR, NRR
- Net MRR churn rate (ChartMogul) = (K + X − E − R) ÷ MRR(start) [CMNC, snippet].
- Net MRR retention (ChartMogul) = (MRR(start) + E + R − X − K) ÷ MRR(start); "Excludes New
  Business"; Starting MRR = Active or Past due at period start; option "Exclude reactivations from
  numerator … will lower your retention numbers"; WoW/MoM/QoQ/YoY toggles [CMN]. Period-based,
  not a fixed cohort.
- SaaS Capital (cohort, annual): NRR = "Monthly Recurring Revenue in December of 2024 only from
  customers who were customers in December 2023 ÷ Total MRR in December 2023"; GRR = same,
  "excluding the upsells, cross-sells, and price increases … set each customer's [later] MRR to be
  less than or equal to their [earlier] MRR … gross retention cannot exceed 100%" [SC].
  → GRR is capped **per customer**, not by capping the total at 100%.
- Stripe cohort revenue retention: cohort by first positive MRR; "can exceed 100%" [SA].
- a16z: revenue churn "measured annually … blends upsells with churn"; "net revenue churn
  understates the losses" [A16] → always show GRR beside NRR.
- Disagreement: period-based NRR (ChartMogul) credits reactivations of customers who were not in
  the start base; cohort NRR (SaaS Capital) counts only start-cohort customers' end MRR (a
  customer who left and returned inside the year counts at its end MRR; a new logo never counts).

### 2.6 Monthly vs annual (compounding)
- Stripe cohort example: 10% monthly subscriber churn → month 1 90%, month 2 81%, month 3 73%,
  month 4 66%, month 5 59% [SA]: retention compounds; 12 × 10% = 120% annual churn is impossible.
- Annual churn from a steady monthly rate m: 1 − (1 − m)^12 (convention; consistent with [SA]'s
  compounding; m = 2% → 21.5%, not 24%). Preferred: measure the 12-month figure directly on the
  start-of-year cohort [SC] rather than converting.
- Never annualise a partial month or a single month's movements (a16z run-rate warning [A16]).

### 2.7 Cohort retention (logo and revenue)
- Cohort = customers grouped by first subscription start (ChartMogul: "day, week, month, quarter,
  or year in which a customer began their first subscription" [CMCO]) or first positive MRR
  (Stripe [SA]). Logo: active count at end of each later month ÷ cohort start count; revenue:
  cohort MRR at end of month k ÷ cohort start MRR (can exceed 100%) [SA].
- Churn can be measured vs previous interval or vs starting cohort value [CMCO].
- Young cohorts are right-censored: show only completed months (**unverified**, convention).

### 2.8 ARPA / ARPU
- Stripe ARPU = total MRR ÷ total active subscribers (end-of-period example: $50,000 ÷ 100 = $500)
  [SA]. ChartMogul ARPA = "Total MRR ÷ Number of paying subscribers"; free subscriptions excluded
  [CMA]. Numerator and denominator at the **same date**; ARPU vs ARPA differ when one account has
  many users (vendor glossaries, **unverified**).

### 2.9 LTV
- Stripe: ARPU ÷ subscriber churn rate ($500 ÷ 9% = $5,555) [SA]. ChartMogul: ARPA ÷ "Six-month
  trailing average of Customer Churn Rate", "excluding the current month"; not computed when churn
  is zero; "fluctuates more … for businesses with fewer paying customers" [CMLT]. ChartMogul's
  metrics library variant multiplies by gross margin (snippet, chartmogul.com/metrics/ltv).
- a16z: LTV "is the present value of the future net profit"; "A common mistake is to estimate the
  LTV as a present value of revenue or even gross margin" [A16].
- Caveats: 1/churn assumes constant churn forever; a small churn denominator explodes the result.
  → **Needs gross margin** for any profit-based LTV; a subscription file only supports a labelled
  "revenue LTV estimate".

### 2.10 CAC, CAC payback, magic number, Rule of 40, quick ratio
| Metric | Definition | Needs data a subscription file lacks |
|---|---|---|
| CAC | S&M cost ÷ new customers; a16z: "blended" (incl. organic) vs paid, paid "more important" [A16] | S&M spend |
| CAC payback (months) | S&M (prior period) ÷ (new MRR × gross margin %); denominator = new-logo MRR, not net new (vendor/secondary, **unverified**; Bessemer decks not read) | S&M, gross margin |
| Magic number | "annualized new (GAAP) revenue as a percentage of last quarter's Sales & Marketing expense"; assumes "prior quarter's spending impacts the next quarter's sales growth" [SVP] | S&M, GAAP revenue |
| Rule of 40 | "growth plus profitability should be 40% or greater once at scale (double digit millions of revenue)" (Feld via [R40]); margin measure (EBITDA vs FCF) varies (**unverified**) | profit margin |
| Quick ratio | (N + E) ÷ (K + X), attributed to Mamoon Hamid; some include R in the numerator (SaaStr transcript, snippet) | — (computable) |
| Gross margin | gross profit ÷ revenue; costs incl. "manufacturing, delivery, and support" [A16] | COGS |

### 2.11 Recognised revenue vs billings vs bookings vs MRR
- a16z: "Bookings is the value of a contract"; "Revenue is recognized when the service is
  actually provided or ratably over the life of the subscription agreement"; cash collected at
  booking goes to "a liability line item called deferred revenue" [A16].
- ASC 606 / IFRS 15: fixed-fee SaaS is typically a stand-ready obligation satisfied over time
  (606-10-25-27, customer "simultaneously receives and consumes" the benefit) and usually a series
  [DT, snippet]; straight-line time-based recognition "will be reasonable in many cases" but is
  not an automatic default [PWQ, snippet].
- Stripe RevRec: "When the invoice finalizes, Revenue Recognition defers the total recognizable
  amount and subsequently amortizes it evenly over the period of each invoice line item"; deferred
  revenue = "Services that have been invoiced but not recognized as revenue … a liability";
  recognizable revenue excludes tax (90 of a 100 USD invoice with 10 tax) [SR].
- → A $1,200 annual prepaid invoice = $1,200 billings in month 1, $100 MRR for 12 months [BM, SA],
  ≈$100 recognised revenue per month, $1,100 deferred revenue after month 1 [SR]. MRR ≠ revenue:
  MRR excludes one-time fees (which are revenue) and is a point-in-time run rate; revenue is a
  period flow under GAAP [A16, SS].

## 3. Benchmarks (context only, never a verdict)
- **SaaS Capital 2025** (14th survey, Q1, >1,000 private B2B SaaS, excludes < $1M ARR): median NRR
  101%, GRR 91%; GRR 95% for ACV > $250k vs ~91% below; median NRR by ACV 98% (< $12k), 103%,
  102%, 104%, 102%, 106% (> $250k); bootstrapped 104%/92%, equity-backed 101%/90%; multi-year
  contracts 103%/94%, annual 101%/90%, monthly 100%/89%; median growth 24%; "GRR must be at least
  90%" for peer parity [SC].
- **ChartMogul (Dec 2025, 3,500 companies)**: "median NRR for B2B SaaS is 82%, with the top quartile
  reaching 97%"; B2C and AI-native ~48–49% [CMB]. → 82% vs SaaS Capital 101%: different
  populations (self-serve, small ARPA included vs ≥ $1M ARR survey) and methods (billing data vs
  self-report). Never quote one benchmark without its population.
- **KeyBanc/Sapphire**: NRR 108% (2022) → 104% (2023); median CAC payback ~23 months (2022);
  ARR growth 35% → 26% (> 100 companies, median ARR $25.5M) [KB23]. 2025 edition (2025-11-13):
  gross retention 86% in 2023, "expected to approach the 90% threshold"; NRR "above 100%"; ARR
  growth 15% (2024) → 20% (2025 expected) [KB25].
- Benchmarks are self-reported, survivor-biased, and use each respondent's own definition.

## 4. Traps (with the authority that warns)
1. Summing invoice totals as MRR [SS]; counting setup/one-time fees [A16, CMM, PF].
2. An annual prepaid invoice as 12× MRR in its month (or as that month's revenue) [BM, SR].
3. Bookings or billings × 12 called ARR [A16].
4. MRR called revenue; MRR excludes one-time revenue and is not GAAP [A16, SR].
5. Monthly churn × 12 as annual churn; compounding [SA].
6. Churn denominator including in-period new customers (Stripe 9.1%) vs start-only (10%) without
   saying which [SA, CMC].
7. Trials, free plans or 100%-coupon accounts in paying counts or ARPA [SA, PF, BM, CMA].
8. Past-due treated three ways: in MRR (Stripe, ChartMogul), churned after 30 days (Baremetrics),
   delinquent churn (ProfitWell) [SA, CMM, BM, PF].
9. Churn dated at cancellation request vs end of paid period [PS, CML].
10. A plan swap via cancel + new subscription read as Churn + New instead of Expansion (or as
    Churn + Reactivation by ChartMogul's rule) [CMV]: classify per customer.
11. Partial cancellation (one of several subscriptions) counted as logo churn [CMV].
12. Discounts: applying/expiring one moves MRR (contraction/expansion) [CMV]; Stripe finite-duration
    coupons left MRR unaffected on the older page [PS] but are configurable today [SA].
13. FX swings recorded as expansion/contraction [CMF]; Stripe reports them as an FX line [SA].
14. Usage/metered revenue mixed into MRR (Stripe out [SA], ProfitWell in [PC]).
15. NRR without GRR [A16]; GRR capped on the total instead of per customer [SC].
16. Period NRR (incl. reactivations of non-cohort customers) compared with a cohort benchmark [CMN, SC].
17. Same-month signup+churn: in (Stripe) or out (ProfitWell) of new and churn [PS].
18. LTV from revenue presented as profit [A16]; LTV with ~0 churn [CMLT].
19. Extrapolated partial-period churn [CMC] or a partial month annualised.
20. Rows (subscription items) counted as customers [SA, CMA].
21. Snapshot `Churn = Yes` share read as a monthly churn rate (no period).
22. Benchmark used as a verdict; populations differ (§3).

## 5. Must say / must never say (senior SaaS finance / RevOps lead)
Must say: the as-of date and period; MRR's inclusion rules (recurring only, net of discounts, ex
tax, trials/free excluded, annual ÷ 12, usage in or out, past-due rule); the customer counting
unit (customer, not subscription row); the churn denominator (customers / MRR at period start);
whether a retention figure is cohort-based or period-based and over which window; GRR alongside
NRR; the churn timing rule (end of paid period vs cancellation); the reactivation rule (no window,
or N days); FX method if multi-currency; that benchmarks are context with their population named.

Must never say:
- MRR or ARR "is revenue" / "is recognised revenue" [A16, SR]; billings or bookings as revenue.
- An annual churn or ARR figure from one month × 12 when the month is partial or churn is monthly [SA, A16].
- A churn rate whose denominator includes customers acquired in the period without saying so [SA, CMC].
- Trial, free or 100%-discount accounts as paying customers [SA, PF].
- LTV, CAC, payback, magic number, Rule of 40 or gross margin from a file without cost, S&M or
  margin columns (state "needs S&M spend / gross margin").
- "Healthy", "good" or "bad" from a benchmark comparison alone (§3).
- Causal claims ("price increase caused churn") from observational subscription data.

## 7. Word collisions with other domains
- **Churn**: telco/retail customer churn, HR attrition; insurance lapse.
- **Retention**: HR retention, insurance renewal retention, this repo's `RETENTION_DAYS`.
- **Revenue**: retail sales revenue (a flow) vs MRR (a run rate).
- **Subscription / member**: health-plan member, gym membership, newspaper subscriber.
- **Plan**: insurance plan, health plan.
- **Cohort**: healthcare/HR cohorts.
- Detection rule: customer id + (plan | interval | MRR) + start date + (status | end date) ⇒ SaaS.
