## 1. Grain of typical extracts

- **Snapshot roster** (one row per employee as of an extract date, status Active/Terminated):
  the most common upload; hire + termination dates allow period flows; without them only the
  point-in-time share is computable.
- **Effective-dated job history** (one row per person per job change): SAP SuccessFactors EmpJob
  key `userId + startDate + seqNumber` (vendor blog, snippet:
  https://truto.one/blog/connect-sap-successfactors-to-ai-agents-automate-talent-odata-ops.md).
  A row is a job slice, not a person: headcount = distinct person at a date.
- **Event/transaction table** (hire, termination, transfer, promotion rows). Workday builds
  termination reports on the Business Process Transactions data source (ServiceNow doc, snippet:
  https://www.servicenow.com/docs/r/oZRGKGLbfcn968v3Ubchmw/r2W7noc~4hy5MSc5ROR92g).
- **Monthly headcount snapshots** (person × month): Workday's averages are built on this
  ("rollingSum(unique([Employee_ID]), 12) … / 12") [WD].
- **IBM HR Attrition sample** (fictional, 1,470 rows × 35 cols): one row per employee,
  `Attrition` = Yes/No, **no dates**. Columns include `Age, Attrition, BusinessTravel, Department,
  EmployeeNumber, Gender, JobLevel, JobRole, MaritalStatus, MonthlyIncome, OverTime,
  PerformanceRating, YearsAtCompany, YearsInCurrentRole, YearsSinceLastPromotion,
  YearsWithCurrManager` (header read from
  https://raw.githubusercontent.com/IBM/employee-attrition-aif360/master/data/emp_attrition.csv).
  → Trap: its "attrition rate" (≈16%) is a share of a sample with no period, not an annual
  turnover rate.

## 2. KPI definitions (exact)

Notation: S = separations in period, H = hires, HC(t) = headcount at date t, AvgHC = average
headcount over period.

### 2.1 Headcount (point-in-time)
- Unit: **person** (distinct employee id) active on a date. Workday "Active Headcount" =
  `unique([Employee_ID]) for ("Active_Status","True")` [WD].
- UK GPG: "Your 'headcount' is of the number of individual employees you have, not full-time
  equivalents"; part-timers and job-sharers count as one; include casual, temporary, apprentice,
  zero-hours employees on the snapshot date; **exclude agency workers** ("part of the headcount of
  the agency that provides them") [GPGW].
- JOLTS employment: "all persons on the payroll who worked during or received pay for the pay
  period that includes the 12th of the month"; paid leave included; unpaid leave or strike for the
  whole pay period excluded; temp-agency, leasing, contractor, consultant workers counted by their
  employer of record [JD, JC].
- Period basis: HC **at period end**, the date stated (consistent with the GPG snapshot date
  [GPG reg.1(2)] and Workday "as of the previous period" [WD]).

### 2.2 FTE
- "Full-time equivalent employment is the number of full-time equivalent jobs, defined as total
  hours worked divided by average annual hours worked in full-time jobs"
  (https://unstats.un.org/unsd/nationalaccount/glossresults.asp?gID=202).
- HRIS FTE is usually contracted hours ÷ standard full-time hours (SuccessFactors `fte`,
  `standardHours`; https://help.okta.com/en-us/Content/Topics/Provisioning/sfec/sfec-entities-attributes.htm)
  — **unverified** as a universal rule.
- Never sum FTE and headcount; rates are conventionally on headcount (SHRM, snippet of
  https://shrm.org/topics-tools/tools/how-to-guides/how-to-determine-turnover-rate).

### 2.3 Average headcount
- (a) (HC_start + HC_end) / 2: Workday Monthly Attrition [WD]; SHRM monthly method (snippet).
- (b) Mean of month-end (monthly) headcounts: Workday Average Active Headcount = rolling sum of 12
  monthly actives ÷ 12 [WD].
- (c) Mean of daily headcount: **unverified** (analyst convention).
- JOLTS annual rates use Σ of 12 monthly employment levels as denominator [JA, JF].
- (a) and (b) diverge whenever headcount moves inside the period (a layoff, a hiring spike):
  state the method.

### 2.4 Separations / turnover / attrition
- JOLTS separations = "all separations from the payroll during the entire reference month":
  **quits** ("left voluntarily with the exception of retirements or transfers to other
  locations"), **layoffs and discharges** ("involuntary separations initiated by the employer"),
  **other separations** ("retirements, transfers to other locations, separations due to disability
  and deaths") [JC, JD]. → Retirement is **not** a quit; many HRIS put it under "voluntary".
- Monthly rate: "dividing the number of [hires/separations] by employment … multiplying that
  quotient by 100" [JK].
- Workday: Monthly Attrition = unique terminated ids ÷ avg(begin, end) HC × 100; 12-Month Rolling
  = rolling 12-month terminations ÷ 12-month average active HC × 100; voluntary/involuntary by
  `Termination_Category` [WD].
- Counting unit: JOLTS counts **events**; Workday counts `unique([Employee_ID])` [WD]. Sources
  disagree: pick events and state it.
- Regrettable turnover: no standards-body definition (**unverified**, vendor convention).
- "Turnover" vs "attrition": no authority distinguishes them consistently [WD]: synonyms, state
  the formula.

### 2.5 Annualisation
- JOLTS (since Jan 2023 data): annual average rates = Σ 12 monthly levels ÷ Σ 12 monthly
  employment × 100; "Annual rates are computed as annual averages, instead of annual totals"
  [JA, JF]. 2025 annual average total separations rate = 3.3% (snippet of JA): a **monthly-average**
  rate.
- SHRM: annual turnover by "adding each of the monthly rates together" (snippet,
  https://shrm.org/resourcesandtools/tools-and-samples/hr-qa/pages/turnovercalculations.aspx).
- Workday Annualized Attrition = FYTD terminations ÷ FYTD avg active HC × (12 / months elapsed)
  [WD]: linear.
- Compounding 1 − (1 − m)^12: no primary HR authority found (**unverified**).
- → Pack rule: S over the stated window ÷ AvgHC; annualise only on request, linearly (×12/n),
  labelled.

### 2.6 Retention rate
- Cohort definition (convention, **unverified**): of HC at period start, share still employed at
  period end; in-period hires excluded. Retention ≠ 1 − turnover.
- Workday "Retention Rate" differs (start HC − terminations + hires …) [WD]. Name the formula.

### 2.7 New-hire (first-year) turnover
- Exits within 12 months of hire ÷ hires in the cohort window; cohorts < 12 months old are
  right-censored (**unverified**).

### 2.8 Hires and hire rate
- JOLTS hires = "all additions to the payroll during the entire reference month", **including
  rehires, recalls … and transfers in from other locations**; excludes transfers or promotions
  within the establishment [JC, JD]. Rate = H ÷ employment × 100 [JK].

### 2.9 Internal mobility / promotion rate
- No public standard; Workday "IC to Manager Rate" = 12-month count ÷ 12-month avg active HC ×
  100 [WD]. Internal moves are not separations for the organisation [JC].

### 2.10 Transfers
- At establishment level JOLTS records a transfer-out as an other separation for the losing
  location and a hire for the gaining one [JC, JD]; at company level neither.

### 2.11 Rehires
- JOLTS: a rehire is a new hire event [JC]. Workday: Hire Date ("most recent hire or rehire") vs
  Original Hire Date (snippet: https://dbm.maryland.gov/sps/Documents/Workday_Date_Field_Guide.pdf);
  BambooHR `hireDate` and `originalHireDate` (https://documentation.bamboohr.com/docs/list-of-field-names).
  → One person, two spells: headcount counts the person once; separations count each exit.

### 2.12 Tenure
- BLS: "how long wage and salary workers had been with their current employer"; median = "the
  point at which half of all workers had more tenure and half had less" [TN]. Median tenure can
  rise in recessions because "less-senior workers are more likely to lose their jobs" [TN].
  → Current-staff tenure is an incomplete spell (a lower bound); tenure at exit uses completed
  spells; report medians, labelled.
- Workday Average Tenure = Σ length of service of actives ÷ active HC [WD].

### 2.13 Time to fill / time to hire
- Workday: Time to Fill = DAYS_BETWEEN(recruiting start, requisition filled); Time to Hire =
  DAYS_BETWEEN(recruiting start, worker hire) [WD].

### 2.14 Offer acceptance rate
- Accepted ÷ extended × 100; pending offers handled differently by vendors (**unverified**).

### 2.15 Absence rate and Bradford factor
- Lost time ÷ available time × 100 (HSE Ireland 2026 instructions, snippet). Bradford factor =
  S² × D (snippet); mechanical use on disability- or pregnancy-related absence risks indirect
  discrimination (Equality Act 2010 s.20; secondary sources). Never rank individuals by it.

### 2.16 Span of control
- Workday Average Span of Control = Σ direct reports of managers with reports ÷ those managers [WD].

### 2.17 Compa-ratio
- Salary ÷ range midpoint; Workday Average Compa-Ratio = Σ Compa_Ratio of actives ÷ active HC
  [WD]. Requires a midpoint column.

### 2.18 Pay gap
- **UK** (SI 2017/172): 250+ employees on the snapshot date [GPG reg.1(2), GPGD]; mean and median
  hourly-pay gap of full-pay relevant employees, bonus gaps, quartiles [GPG reg.2(1)]; mean gap =
  (A − B) ÷ A × 100, A = male mean hourly pay [GPG reg.8]; ordinary pay excludes overtime,
  redundancy pay, benefits in kind [GPG reg.3]; hourly rate rules [GPG reg.6–7]; quartiles
  [GPG reg.13].
- **EU** (PTD): "'gender pay gap' means the difference in average pay levels between female and
  male workers … expressed as a percentage of the average pay level of male workers" [Art.3(1)(c)];
  pay level = gross annual and gross hourly [Art.3(1)(b)–(e)]; joint pay assessment if the gap is
  "at least 5 % in any category of workers" and unjustified [Art.10(1)]: per category, not
  organisation-wide.
- **Adjusted** gap: not a legal reporting metric. EEOC §10 warns factors like prior salary "can
  reflect sex-based compensation discrimination"
  (https://www.eeoc.gov/laws/guidance/section-10-compensation-discrimination, §III.A.3.c, IV.F.2.g).

### 2.19 Contingent workers
- JOLTS, UK GPG: agency, leasing, contractor, consultant workers belong to the employer of record
  and are excluded [JD, GPGW]. Interns, seasonal and temporary **employees** stay in (GPGW; JOLTS
  counts seasonal terminations as layoffs [JC]).

### 2.20 ISO 30414
- 2025 edition replaces 2018; 11 areas incl. workforce composition, diversity, recruitment,
  mobility and succession, **workforce turnover** (AFNOR catalogue:
  https://www.boutique.afnor.org/en-gb/standard/iso-304142025/human-resource-management-requirements-and-recommendations-for-human-capita/xs146249/449389).
  Formulas **unverified** (paywalled).

## 3. Traps (with the authority that warns)
1. Snapshot "attrition" (share of rows with left=1) as a period rate (IBM sample §1); our own
   `semantic_layers/hr.yaml` had `attrition_rate = AVG(employees.left)`.
2. A JOLTS annual average monthly rate (≈3.3%) compared with a company's annual rate [JA, JF].
3. Summing monthly rates vs annual S ÷ AvgHC (SHRM vs
   https://www.numericalinsights.com/blog/how-to-choose-a-turnover-definition-and-correctly-measure-retention).
4. End-of-period HC as denominator (https://www.metabase.com/metrics/employee-turnover-rate, snippet).
5. Retirements counted as quits [JC].
6. Transfers counted as company separations [JC].
7. A rehire as two persons, or tenure reset silently [JC; Workday date guides].
8. Contractors or agency workers in headcount [JD, GPGW].
9. Unpaid leave of absence (JOLTS excludes whole-period unpaid leave [JD]; UK GPG keeps leave in
   the headcount, pay gap over full-pay employees only [GPGW, GPG reg.1(2)]).
10. Current-staff tenure vs tenure at exit [TN].
11. Annual salary gap with part-time staff; the UK uses hourly pay [GPG reg.6].
12. Overtime or redundancy pay in ordinary pay [GPG reg.3(2)].
13. An org-wide gap < 5% read as "no joint pay assessment" [PTD Art.10(1)(a)].
14. Adjusted gap controlling for a tainted factor [EEOC §10].
15. Bradford factor on disability absence (Equality Act s.20).
16. Span of control including ICs [WD].
17. Effective-dated rows counted as people.
18. Future-dated terminations (Workday integrations may omit them, snippet:
    https://systematic.workato.com/t5/community-chat/workday-web-service-unable-to-fetch-data-for-workers-who-are/m-p/7073):
    use termination_date ≤ as-of.
19. Right-censored cohorts (**unverified**).

## 4. Must say / must never say
Must say: the period, as-of date, denominator method, counting unit (events vs persons), what is
excluded (contractors, transfers, retirements), the annualisation method if any.

Must never say:
- **Causal claims from observational data** ("low salary causes attrition"): EEOC §10 frames even
  regression as association after controls.
- **Individual-level predictions or rankings** (flight-risk scores, Bradford league tables): EU AI
  Act Annex III point 4(b) lists as high-risk "AI systems intended to be used to make decisions
  affecting … the promotion or termination of work-related contractual relationships … or to
  monitor and evaluate the performance and behaviour of persons"
  (http://publications.europa.eu/resource/celex/32024R1689); GDPR Art.22.
- **Recommendations keyed on protected attributes**: UK DPA 2018 Sch.1 para 8 allows equality
  monitoring only to review equality, not "for measures or decisions about a particular data
  subject"
  (https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2018/12/schedule/1/part/2/crossheading/equality-of-opportunity-or-treatment);
  PTD Art.12(2).
- **Small groups**: PTD Art.12(3) (information that "would lead to the disclosure … of the pay of
  an identifiable worker"); ONS SRS "low count threshold of 10" (DfE/ONS SDC policy, snippet:
  https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/660d8798758315001a4a49d2/DfE_ONS_statistical_disclosure_control_policy.pdf);
  KYSTATS: no data "for any groups with less than 10 individuals" (snippet); the threshold is
  contested (https://uwe-repository.worktribe.com/OutputFile/7457513).
  → Suppress pay, demographic, absence or performance cells under the product's small-cell
  threshold (11: 1–10 suppressed).

## 5. Personal data and special categories
- GDPR Art.9(1) special categories: "racial or ethnic origin, political opinions, religious or
  philosophical beliefs, or trade union membership", genetic, biometric, "data concerning health",
  sex life or sexual orientation (https://gdpr-info.eu/art-9-gdpr/); Art.9(2)(b) employment
  exception needs Union/Member State law with safeguards; Art.88 (https://gdpr-info.eu/art-88-gdpr/);
  Art.10 criminal data.
- US: ADA medical information "must be collected and maintained on separate forms and in separate
  medical files" (https://www.eeoc.gov/laws/guidance/enforcement-guidance-preemployment-disability-related-questions-and-medical);
  FMLA certifications are confidential medical records (29 CFR 825.500(g),
  https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/29/825.500). SSN is a direct identifier.
- Classification:
  - direct identifiers (mask): name, email, phone, home address, SSN/NI number, date of birth,
    bank account, passport/visa document numbers;
  - quasi-identifiers: employee id; department × level × gender × age band (small cells);
  - special category: ethnicity/race, disability, health, sickness-absence reason, leave-reason
    codes, union membership, religion, sexual orientation;
  - ordinary but sensitive in use: salary, bonus, performance rating, termination reason, gender,
    age, marital status.
- Decision in the plan (Domain 5, 2026-11-04): PII mode (direct identifiers tokenised and
  pseudonymised, ADR-0008 §1) plus small-cell suppression by distinct people for groups by a
  protected role or of pay, rather than the PHI working copy (which would withhold job titles and
  flag every small count while missing two-way pay cells).

## 7. Word collisions with other domains
- **Retention**: insurance renewal retention; SaaS/retail customer retention; HR cohort
  retention; this repo's `RETENTION_DAYS`.
- **Churn / attrition**: customer churn vs employee turnover; a `customer_id` + churn flag is a
  customer table.
- **Termination**: insurance policy termination/expiry; HR separation.
- **Claims**: benefit claims inside HR extracts (health data); a claim id + reserve ⇒ insurance.
- **Member**: health-plan member, union member (Art.9), club member.
- **Position**, **tenure** (customer months on book), **grade** (credit grade).
- Detection rule: hire date + (termination date | status) + department/job/manager ⇒ HR.
